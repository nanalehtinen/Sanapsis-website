Compared on 5 Oct 2026: the English site texts (build.py) against your new Finnish texts.
The "Finnish says" lines are my own back-translations into English, so they are not wording to copy. I have not changed any English text.

Priority: **A** = the content differs (facts or a missing message), **B** = smaller differences in tone or detail.

## Home page
- **B. Intro.** EN: "Two separate apps, each supporting a different part of the therapy journey." Finnish says: the *Sanapsis family* has two separate apps that support speech-therapy *practice* in different ways. Only the angle differs.

## SanapsisPro page
- **A. Intro (3 paragraphs in EN, 1 in FI).** The Finnish focuses on supporting the *planning and carrying out* of adult neurological speech therapy: a wide range of exercises, ideas for planning therapy, and material for doing the exercises with the client.
  - EN has things the Finnish no longer says: "iPad", "acquired communication difficulties", "developed and tested for adult speech and language therapy", "in the same download".
- **A. New SanapsisLite paragraph is missing in EN.** Finnish says: you can get to know the idea of SanapsisPro by downloading SanapsisLite, which has example exercises in three languages.
- **A. Get box.** The Finnish starts with "Sanapsis is a one-time purchase." EN doesn't say this. The EN starts with "iPad.", which the Finnish dropped. ⚠ The Finnish Pro page now doesn't say "iPad" anywhere. Do you want it back there?
- **A. Semantics tile.** The Finnish dropped "which works especially well with high-level TBI patients". The EN still has it.
- **A. Note box.** The Finnish adds a last sentence: if you are looking for support for independent practice, have a look at Sanapsis+. EN doesn't have it.
  - (B) The heading also differs: "Important information" in EN, "Huomaathan!" (Please note!) in FI.
