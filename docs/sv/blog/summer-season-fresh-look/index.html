summer season, fresh look

21 June 2026 · Nana Lehtinen · SanapsisPro

In Finland, there is an expression about shedding your “winter fur” when you take the first swim of the summer. It is a small seasonal reset after the long, dark winter months and a playful transition into the summer season full of light and color.

Midsummer is a popular time for that first swim. This year, SanapsisPro joined on the fun!

As midsummer rolled around, SanapsisPro jumped into the deep end and a whole new visual look emerged - fresher, lighter, and more energetic. The new design brings brightness and ease to the app while keeping the core experience familiar: clear tasks, simple navigation, and practical support for everyday speech therapy. Dive in and take a look!

As you can see, the language switcher now sits in the top corner. The same look applies across all languages, and below you can see a few examples from each language.
